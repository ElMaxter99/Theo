# Brand artwork notice

The files in this folder (mascot, application icon, favicon, symbol) are **provisional** brand artwork copied from
`docs/brand/assets/` of the Theo workspace (round 2, generated with an AI image tool and vectorised; see BRAND-009 and
Q-026 there).

- They are **NOT covered by the MIT licence** of this repository. All rights reserved; the final licence and a usage policy
  will be written with the final artwork.
- They are **provisional and under review**: the ownership and registrability of AI-generated images is uncertain.
- They are meant to be replaced. The interface references them only through `<img src="brand/...">`, so swapping a file with the
  same name (or editing the paths in `web/src/brand.ts`) is enough.
