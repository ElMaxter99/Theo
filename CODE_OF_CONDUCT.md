# Code of conduct

Theo is for people who want to get work done without drama. In this repository, issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when you do not understand something.
- Keep feedback concrete and kind. Harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not accepted.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a conduct problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (the **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**), mention that it is a conduct report, and it will be handled privately.
